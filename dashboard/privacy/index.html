Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Contact: support@freesurf.tools

This Privacy Policy explains how Catpix collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Catpix website, web app, mobile apps, and API. Catpix is a social media scheduling and cross-posting service and is part of the FreeSurf family of tools. Other FreeSurf products have their own privacy policies.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. If you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials through Supabase. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address associated with that account. We use this information to recognize you and, if you opt in, to send product updates.

Social media connections. When you connect a social account, we receive and store the information the platform provides for that connection:

  • Your account identifier, username, display name, and profile image (if provided)
  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to act on your behalf
  • The permissions or scopes you granted
  • Basic account information needed to show the connection

We do not receive or store your social media password. We use these connections only to schedule and publish content at your direction, display connected accounts, and fetch analytics you request.

Content and scheduling data. When you create, schedule, or publish a post, we store the text, media references, first comments, scheduling details, platform selections, drafts, and the results or error messages returned by the platform. If you use analytics features, we may receive engagement data that the platform makes available through its API.

Payment information. Catpix is free to use. Some platforms charge fees that we pass through at cost, such as X's per-post fees. If you buy posting credits, payments are processed by Stripe. We receive transaction details such as the payment status, amount, and a customer identifier. We do not receive or store your full card number.

Technical and support data. We collect basic server logs, such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, and error details, to keep Catpix secure and troubleshoot problems. If you contact support, we retain your correspondence so we can help and document the outcome.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve Catpix
  • Authenticate you and secure your account
  • Connect social accounts and publish or schedule content at your direction
  • Show post history, scheduling status, and analytics you request
  • Process posting credits and calculate platform fees
  • Respond to support requests
  • Send optional product updates if you opt in
  • Detect abuse, enforce our terms, and comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your content for advertising, and Catpix does not run third-party advertising or cross-site tracking.

3. Legal Bases

If you are in the EEA or UK, we process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide Catpix, connect your accounts, and publish content you request
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot, and improve Catpix
  • Consent: for optional product updates, where we ask for it
  • Legal obligation: for tax, accounting, and lawful requests

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Catpix uses browser storage for essential functions such as keeping you signed in, remembering your theme preference, and storing onboarding choices. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. You can clear browser storage through your browser settings, though you may need to sign in again.

5. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to operate Catpix:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
  • Cloudflare — website hosting, Workers, and storage
  • Bundle.social — social media API provider used to connect accounts and publish content while direct platform integrations are being rolled out
  • Stripe — payment processing for posting credits
  • Social networks you connect — receive the content you publish and provide account and, where enabled, analytics data through their APIs
  • Professional advisers, authorities, or courts — when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or to investigate abuse

These providers process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own terms. We do not sell your social media data.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained while your account is active. You can request deletion by contacting us.
  • Social connections and tokens: retained while the account is connected. Disconnecting deletes the stored token; deleting your Catpix account deletes your connections.
  • Posts and schedules: retained until you delete them or delete your account, unless needed to resolve a dispute or comply with law.
  • Payment and tax records: retained as required by applicable law.
  • Server logs: retained for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.
  • Support requests: retained to resolve issues and document outcomes.

Backup copies may persist briefly after deletion before they are overwritten or expire.

7. Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections, and reputable infrastructure providers to protect your information. No service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your Catpix login credentials confidential and for reviewing which social accounts you connect.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can disconnect a social account at any time, which removes the stored OAuth token. Deleting your Catpix account removes your connections and associated scheduling data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@freesurf.tools. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know and delete; we do not sell personal information.

9. International Transfers

Catpix is operated from the United States, and our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

10. Children's Privacy

Catpix is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be noted with an updated effective date. Continued use of Catpix after changes means you accept the updated policy.
