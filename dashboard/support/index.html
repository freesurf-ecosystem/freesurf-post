Support

Need help with FreeSurf Post? The fastest way to reach us is by email. We reply within one business day.

Contact us
For account, publishing, billing, or technical questions:
support@freesurf.tools
Please include your account email, the platform involved, and a brief description of the issue (and a link to the post if one is affected).

Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account or my data?

To delete your FreeSurf Post account, email support@freesurf.tools from the address you signed up with and we will remove it for you. You can also delete your account from the app. See our Privacy Policy for details on what we store and how to request deletion.

How do I connect a social account?

Open the Accounts tab, choose the platform, and complete the provider's sign-in flow. For Facebook, LinkedIn, and YouTube, select the Page or Channel you want to publish to. Instagram requires a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page. You can disconnect an account at any time from the same tab.

How do I publish or schedule a post?

Open Compose, select the platforms, add your text and media, then publish now or choose a date and time. Scheduled posts are processed every five minutes. YouTube and TikTok accept a single video per post; other platforms may accept multiple images or a carousel where supported.

Why did my post fail?

Common causes are an expired or revoked connection, missing Page/Channel permissions, unsupported media (type, size, or aspect ratio), platform rate limits, or insufficient X credits. Reconnect the account, check the platform's requirements, and try again. If it still fails, email us with the platform, the post text, and the approximate time so we can investigate.

How does pricing work?

FreeSurf Post is free to use. There is no subscription, per-seat, or per-channel fee. X is the only metered network: its API costs are passed through at cost ($0.015 for a plain post, $0.20 for a post with a link) through optional credits. Everything else is free within platform quotas.

I can't sign in. What should I check?

Try the “Forgot password” link on the sign-in screen and check your spam folder. If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, email us from the address you used to sign up and we will help you regain access.

Does FreeSurf Post have an API?

Yes. The dashboard has an API tab where you can create and revoke keys and see examples. The full reference is at post.freesurf.tools/llms.txt.

Which platforms are supported?

Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit, Discord, Slack, and Google Business Profile. Support varies by platform and may change as the platforms update their APIs.
