What Sprout Social does well

Sprout Social is built for teams that treat social as a serious channel. It combines publishing, a unified inbox, reviews, analytics, and listening. The reporting is deeper than most schedulers, and the inbox is designed for customer care rather than just comments.

Sprout Social pricing

Sprout does not have a free plan. Essentials is $79 per seat per month on annual billing, Standard is $199, Professional is $299, and Advanced is $399. Lower tiers include five profiles; Professional and above unlock unlimited profiles. A 30-day trial is available.

What CatVideo costs

CatVideo is free. There is no per-channel fee, no per-seat fee, and the API is included with an account. X is the only metered network: CatVideo passes X API costs through at cost ($0.015 for a plain post, $0.20 for a post with a link). CatVideo currently supports eight platforms: Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, and YouTube.

Where CatVideo wins

  • No per-channel or per-seat math: adding a platform does not add a new line item.
  • The API is included with an account instead of being an enterprise upgrade.
  • Open source: you can inspect the code, contribute, or self-host later.
  • Android app, while many competitors are web-only or slower on mobile.
  • X API costs passed through at cost instead of bundled into a subscription.

Where Sprout Social still wins

  • Deep reporting and analytics.
  • Unified inbox and reviews management.
  • Social listening add-ons.
  • Approvals and team workflows.
  • Enterprise governance and support.
Let's be honest about the trade-off: Sprout is the better choice for teams that need reporting, inbox, listening, and approvals. CatVideo is a free publishing tool, not an enterprise social suite.

Who should choose which

Choose CatVideo if you mainly need to schedule and publish without per-seat pricing.

Choose Sprout Social if you need reporting depth, inbox, listening, approvals, or enterprise support.

FAQ

Does Sprout Social have a free plan?

No. It offers a 30-day trial.

How many profiles are included?

Lower tiers include five profiles; Professional and above are unlimited.

Is CatVideo a reporting replacement?

No. CatVideo has basic post metrics and trends, not enterprise reporting.

Can CatVideo handle customer replies?

CatVideo supports comment import and replies, but not a full inbox suite.
