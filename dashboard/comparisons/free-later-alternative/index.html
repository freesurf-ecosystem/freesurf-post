What Later does well

Later is built around visual planning. The drag-and-drop calendar, Instagram grid preview, and media library make it easy to plan a week of content visually. It also includes a Link in Bio page, UGC collection, and creator/agency collaboration on higher plans.

Later pricing

Later has no free plan. Starter is $18.75/month on annual billing and caps scheduling at 30 posts per profile per month. Growth is $37.50/month with 180 posts per profile, and Scale is $82.50/month with unlimited posts. Each plan includes a limited number of social sets and users.

What Catpix costs

Catpix is free. There is no per-channel fee, no per-seat fee, and the API is included with an account. X is the only metered network: Catpix passes X API costs through at cost ($0.015 for a plain post, $0.20 for a post with a link). Catpix currently supports eight platforms: Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, and YouTube.

Where Catpix wins

  • No per-channel or per-seat math: adding a platform does not add a new line item.
  • The API is included with an account instead of being an enterprise upgrade.
  • Open source: you can inspect the code, contribute, or self-host later.
  • Android app, while many competitors are web-only or slower on mobile.
  • X API costs passed through at cost instead of bundled into a subscription.

Where Later still wins

  • Visual planner and Instagram grid preview.
  • Link-in-bio page and UGC collection.
  • Creator and agency workflows.
  • Analytics and best-time recommendations.
  • A polished mobile app.
Let's be honest about the trade-off: Later is the better choice when visual planning and link-in-bio are central to your workflow. Catpix is a simpler, free scheduler without a free-tier post cap.

Who should choose which

Choose Catpix if you want free scheduling, an API, and no post caps.

Choose Later if you need visual planning, UGC, link-in-bio, or creator/agency workflows.

FAQ

Does Later have a free plan?

No. Later offers a 14-day trial but no free plan.

What is a social set in Later?

A social set includes one profile from each supported network.

Can Catpix schedule Instagram Reels?

Yes. Catpix supports Instagram posting, including Reels, through its integration.

Does Catpix have link-in-bio?

No. Catpix focuses on scheduling and publishing.
