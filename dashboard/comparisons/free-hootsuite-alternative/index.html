What Hootsuite does well

Hootsuite is one of the original social media management platforms, and it is much broader than a scheduler. It combines publishing, a unified inbox, analytics, competitive benchmarking, and social listening. Standard includes up to 10 social accounts, an inbox, listening, and benchmarking against five competitors. Professional and Advanced add automation, custom reports, approvals, team routing, and deeper listening.

Where Hootsuite stands out is the enterprise workflow: multiple seats, role-based access, approvals, governance, and a mature app directory. It is designed for teams that treat social as an operation, not just a place to queue posts.

Hootsuite plans at a glance

  • Standard ($99/user/mo): up to 10 social accounts, unlimited scheduling, unified inbox, social listening, competitive benchmarking, AI content tools, and 24/7 support.
  • Professional ($199/user/mo): unlimited social accounts, inbox automation, custom reports, deeper listening with 30-day search and 90-day trend forecasting, and more competitor slots.
  • Advanced ($399/user/mo): approval workflows, message assignment and routing, team performance reporting, content collaboration, and advanced reporting exports.
  • Enterprise: SSO, SCIM, compliance controls, advanced listening, employee advocacy, and dedicated support.

Publishing and scheduling

Hootsuite supports a shared content calendar, unlimited scheduling, drafts, bulk uploads, and per-network publishing. It also handles approval workflows on Advanced, so a post can be drafted by one person and approved by another before it goes live. The composer supports platform-specific previews, media, and first comments where the network allows them.

Engagement and inbox

The unified inbox is one of Hootsuite’s strongest features. It brings comments, DMs, and mentions into one place, with assignment, routing, saved replies, and automation on higher tiers. Advanced adds skill-based routing, customer satisfaction surveys, and team productivity reporting, which is significantly deeper than a basic comment inbox.

Analytics, listening, and reporting

Hootsuite includes post and profile analytics, competitive benchmarking, and social listening. Standard covers the basics; Professional adds custom reports, scheduled exports, deeper listening, and trend forecasting. Advanced adds branded reports, cross-team reporting, and more advanced measurement. This is the part of Hootsuite that most free schedulers cannot match.

Governance, approvals, and collaboration

Approvals, roles, access levels, SSO, SCIM, and audit-friendly workflows are where Hootsuite earns its enterprise price. Teams can separate responsibilities, require sign-off, and manage many users without sharing passwords. For a solo creator, these features are mostly overhead; for a large team, they are the reason to pay.

AI and automation

Hootsuite includes AI content generation and recommendations, plus inbox automation on Professional and above. The AI is useful for drafting and repurposing, but it is not the core reason to choose Hootsuite. The core is the workflow and listening stack around it.

Integrations and API

Hootsuite has a broad app directory and a developer platform that includes publishing, analytics, inbox, app, and Amplify APIs, plus MCP servers. Enterprise integrations and governance are more developed than most schedulers. This is another area where Catpix is much simpler: Catpix exposes a free API but does not have an enterprise integration ecosystem.

Hootsuite pricing

Hootsuite does not have a free plan. Standard is $99 per user per month, Professional is $199, and Advanced is $399. Enterprise pricing is custom. The 14-day trial is the only free entry point, and the per-seat model means costs grow with every teammate. For a five-person team on Standard, that is roughly $495/month before add-ons.

What Catpix costs

Catpix is free. There is no per-channel fee, no per-seat fee, and the API is included with an account. X is the only metered network: Catpix passes X API costs through at cost ($0.015 for a plain post, $0.20 for a post with a link). Catpix currently supports eight platforms: Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, and YouTube.

Where Catpix wins

  • No per-channel or per-seat math: adding a platform does not add a new line item.
  • The API is included with an account instead of being an enterprise upgrade.
  • Open source: you can inspect the code, contribute, or self-host later.
  • Android app, while many competitors are web-only or slower on mobile.
  • X API costs passed through at cost instead of bundled into a subscription.

Where Hootsuite still wins

  • Social listening and trend forecasting.
  • Enterprise governance, SSO, and SCIM.
  • Mature approval workflows and team routing.
  • Deeper reporting and competitive benchmarking.
  • A broader enterprise support organization.
Let's be honest about the trade-off: Catpix is not an enterprise listening platform. If you need governance, approvals, or social listening, Hootsuite is the safer choice. Catpix is for people who mainly need to schedule and publish without per-seat pricing.

Who should choose which

Choose Catpix if you are a solo creator or small business that needs free scheduling, an API, and a simple workflow.

Choose Hootsuite if you manage a team, need listening or approvals, or require enterprise governance.
