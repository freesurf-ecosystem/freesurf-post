Free Buffer Alternative

Buffer is polished and simple, but its paid plans are priced per channel. If you post to several networks, the bill climbs quickly. Here is how CatVideo compares, where Buffer still wins, and how to decide.

Updated October 30, 2026 · 8 min read

Short version: Buffer is a good scheduler with a useful free plan. CatVideo is a free, open-source alternative with no per-channel fee and an API included. Buffer is still the better choice if you need a mature inbox, deeper reporting, AI assistance, or approval workflows.

Quick verdict

Choose CatVideo if you post to several networks, want a free API, or do not want to pay per channel. Choose Buffer if you need a polished community inbox, advanced reporting, AI tools, approvals, or a large integration library.

What Buffer does well

Buffer has earned its reputation. The composer is clean, the queue is easy to understand, and the free plan is genuinely usable: three channels and up to 10 scheduled posts per channel. It supports 11 platforms, including Bluesky, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Mastodon, Pinterest, Threads, TikTok, X, YouTube, and Google Business.

Paid plans add unlimited scheduled posts, a hashtag manager, first-comment scheduling, a community inbox, follower growth tracking, advanced analytics, and a Start Page for link-in-bio. The Team plan adds approvals and collaboration. Buffer also exposes an API, even on the free plan, with rate limits that scale by tier.

Buffer pricing in plain English

Buffer prices per connected channel, not per user or per account. The free plan covers three channels. Essentials is $5 per channel per month on annual billing. Team is $10 per channel per month on annual billing.

That sounds cheap at first. It adds up when you post to several networks: five channels on Essentials is about $25/month; ten channels is about $50/month. If you manage multiple brands or clients, the per-channel model becomes the main cost driver.

What CatVideo costs

CatVideo is free. There is no per-channel fee, no per-seat fee, and the API is included with an account. X is the exception: X charges per post through its API, and CatVideo passes those costs through at cost ($0.015 for a plain post, $0.20 for a post with a link).

CatVideo currently supports eight platforms: Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, and YouTube. It includes scheduling, drafts, queues, hashtag groups, saved replies, post metrics, comment import and replies, teams, a web app, an Android app, and an open-source codebase.

Where CatVideo wins

  • No per-channel math. Adding a platform does not add a line item to your bill.
  • Free API. Buffer includes API access, but with request limits by plan. CatVideo includes API keys with an account.
  • Open source. You can inspect the code, self-host later, or contribute.
  • Android app. CatVideo has a live Android app; Buffer also has mobile apps, so this is not a unique advantage.
  • X costs at cost. CatVideo passes X API fees through instead of bundling them into a subscription.

Where Buffer still wins

  • Community inbox. Buffer's inbox is more mature, with filters, saved replies, comment scores, and notifications.
  • Reporting. Buffer has deeper analytics, audience demographics, reports, and benchmarks.
  • AI assistance. Buffer includes AI writing and repurposing tools.
  • Approvals and collaboration. Buffer Team has approval workflows and access levels.
  • Integrations and polish. Buffer integrates with Canva, Google Drive, Dropbox, Unsplash, Zapier, and more, and the UI is very refined.
Let's be honest about the trade-off: CatVideo is not a feature-for-feature Buffer replacement. It is a free cross-poster for people who already know what they want to post and do not need an agency-grade inbox, reporting suite, or approval workflow.

Who should choose which

Choose CatVideo if you are a solo creator, freelancer, or small business posting to several networks, you want a free API, and you care more about scheduling than deep reporting or approvals.

Choose Buffer if you need a polished community inbox, advanced analytics, AI assistance, approvals, or a large integration ecosystem. Buffer is also the safer choice if you want a mature product with a long track record.

FAQ

Does Buffer have a free plan?

Yes. Buffer's free plan includes three channels and up to 10 scheduled posts per channel. Paid plans remove the post cap and add analytics, inbox features, hashtag tools, and approvals.

Is CatVideo really free?

Yes. CatVideo does not charge a subscription or per-channel fee. X API costs are passed through at cost because X charges per post.

Does CatVideo have an API?

Yes. CatVideo includes API keys with an account. The API supports posting, scheduling, media uploads, analytics, comments, teams, and credits.

Can I use Buffer and CatVideo at the same time?

Yes, but avoid scheduling the same post twice. A common approach is to run Buffer for the platforms or workflows it handles better and use CatVideo for free cross-posting and API access.

Which is better for agencies?

Buffer Team is usually the better fit for agencies that need approvals, access levels, and deeper reporting. CatVideo is aimed more at solo users and small teams that want free scheduling.

Does CatVideo support the same platforms as Buffer?

CatVideo currently supports eight platforms. Both tools cover the major networks, but the exact platform lists differ. Check the social media API guides for details.
