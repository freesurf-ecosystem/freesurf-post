What TryPost does well

TryPost combines scheduling with approvals, AI content tools, and team workflows. The Socials plan is $19/month for one workspace; Workspaces is $99/month for unlimited workspaces. The code is AGPL and self-hostable.

TryPost pricing

TryPost Socials is $19/month for one workspace with unlimited accounts and members. Workspaces is $99/month for unlimited workspaces. Self-hosting is free under AGPL. Every plan includes AI, analytics, approvals, API, and MCP.

What CatVideo costs

CatVideo is free. There is no per-channel fee, no per-seat fee, and the API is included with an account. X is the only metered network: CatVideo passes X API costs through at cost ($0.015 for a plain post, $0.20 for a post with a link). CatVideo currently supports eight platforms: Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, and YouTube.

Where CatVideo wins

  • No per-channel or per-seat math: adding a platform does not add a new line item.
  • The API is included with an account instead of being an enterprise upgrade.
  • Open source: you can inspect the code, contribute, or self-host later.
  • Android app, while many competitors are web-only or slower on mobile.
  • X API costs passed through at cost instead of bundled into a subscription.

Where TryPost still wins

  • Approvals and client review.
  • AI content tools.
  • Unlimited members and accounts.
  • Workspace separation.
  • Self-hosting.
Let's be honest about the trade-off: TryPost is the better choice for teams that need approvals, AI, unlimited members, or self-hosting. CatVideo is free and simpler.

Who should choose which

Choose CatVideo if you want free hosted scheduling with a focused workflow.

Choose TryPost if you need approvals, AI, unlimited members, or self-hosting.
