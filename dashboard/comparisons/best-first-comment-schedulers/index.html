Why first comments matter

First comments keep the main caption clean while still giving hashtags, links, or context a place to live. They are especially useful on Instagram and LinkedIn, where captions can get cluttered.

Platform support

Support varies by platform and scheduler. Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn are the most common. TikTok first comments are less consistent. Always check the specific platform in the composer before relying on it.

What CatVideo costs

Where the other tools still win

Let's be honest about the trade-off: CatVideo supports first comments for free, but it does not have the reporting, approvals, or media tooling of the larger suites.

Who should choose which

Choose CatVideo if you want free first-comment scheduling and cross-posting.

Choose a larger suite if you need reporting, approvals, or advanced media tools.
