What Loomly does well

Loomly is built around the content calendar. Ideas, drafts, approvals, and comments live next to each post, making it easy for teams and clients to review work. It also includes a hashtag manager, media library, and post analytics.

Loomly pricing

Loomly Starter is $49/month on annual billing ($65 monthly) for 12 social accounts, three users, and unlimited calendars. Beyond is $249/month annual ($332 monthly) for 60 accounts and unlimited users. There is no free plan.

What CatVideo costs

CatVideo is free. There is no per-channel fee, no per-seat fee, and the API is included with an account. X is the only metered network: CatVideo passes X API costs through at cost ($0.015 for a plain post, $0.20 for a post with a link). CatVideo currently supports eight platforms: Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, and YouTube.

Where CatVideo wins

  • No per-channel or per-seat math: adding a platform does not add a new line item.
  • The API is included with an account instead of being an enterprise upgrade.
  • Open source: you can inspect the code, contribute, or self-host later.
  • Android app, while many competitors are web-only or slower on mobile.
  • X API costs passed through at cost instead of bundled into a subscription.

Where Loomly still wins

  • Approval workflows.
  • Collaborative calendar.
  • Ideas board and content planning.
  • Client-friendly review.
  • Templates and media tools.
Let's be honest about the trade-off: Loomly is the better choice for approval-heavy workflows. CatVideo is for people who want free scheduling without a review process.

Who should choose which

Choose CatVideo if you want free scheduling, API access, and a simple workflow.

Choose Loomly if you need approvals, client review, or a collaborative calendar.

FAQ

No. CatVideo does not have approval workflows.
