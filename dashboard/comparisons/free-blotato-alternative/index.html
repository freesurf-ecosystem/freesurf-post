What Blotato does well

Blotato is built for creators who want to generate content, not just schedule it. It includes AI writing, image and video generation, voiceovers, viral templates, and an API/MCP. It is paid-only, with plans from $29 to $499 per month.

Blotato pricing

Blotato Starter is $29/month for 20 accounts, Creator is $97/month for 40, and Agency is $499/month. There is a 7-day trial but no free plan. AI credits meter generation; scheduling and publishing do not consume credits.

What Catpix costs

Catpix is free. There is no per-channel fee, no per-seat fee, and the API is included with an account. X is the only metered network: Catpix passes X API costs through at cost ($0.015 for a plain post, $0.20 for a post with a link). Catpix currently supports eight platforms: Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, and YouTube.

Where Catpix wins

  • No per-channel or per-seat math: adding a platform does not add a new line item.
  • The API is included with an account instead of being an enterprise upgrade.
  • Open source: you can inspect the code, contribute, or self-host later.
  • Android app, while many competitors are web-only or slower on mobile.
  • X API costs passed through at cost instead of bundled into a subscription.

Where Blotato still wins

  • AI writing and video generation.
  • AI voiceovers and templates.
  • API and MCP for agents.
  • Comment and DM replies.
  • A creator-focused workflow.
Let's be honest about the trade-off: Blotato is the better choice if AI content generation is the point. Catpix is for people who already have content and want free scheduling.

Who should choose which

Choose Catpix if you want free scheduling and API access without AI credits.

Choose Blotato if you want AI writing, video, voice, and a creator content engine.
