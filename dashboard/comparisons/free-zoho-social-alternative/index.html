What Zoho Social does well

Zoho Social works best as part of the Zoho suite. It connects publishing and monitoring to Zoho CRM and Desk, so leads and support tickets can flow from social conversations. Paid tiers add inbox, approvals, custom reports, and monitoring.

Zoho Social pricing

Zoho Social has a free plan for one brand and six channels. Paid plans are Standard, Professional, Premium, Agency, and Agency Plus, with regional pricing. Free includes basic scheduling and reports; deeper inbox, approvals, and monitoring sit in paid tiers.

What Catpix costs

Catpix is free. There is no per-channel fee, no per-seat fee, and the API is included with an account. X is the only metered network: Catpix passes X API costs through at cost ($0.015 for a plain post, $0.20 for a post with a link). Catpix currently supports eight platforms: Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, and YouTube.

Where Catpix wins

  • No per-channel or per-seat math: adding a platform does not add a new line item.
  • The API is included with an account instead of being an enterprise upgrade.
  • Open source: you can inspect the code, contribute, or self-host later.
  • Android app, while many competitors are web-only or slower on mobile.
  • X API costs passed through at cost instead of bundled into a subscription.

Where Zoho Social still wins

  • Zoho CRM and Desk integration.
  • Monitoring and social listening.
  • Agency plans.
  • Value pricing inside Zoho.
  • Custom reports.
Let's be honest about the trade-off: Zoho Social is the better choice for teams already using Zoho. Catpix is for free publishing across more channels with an included API.

Who should choose which

Choose Catpix if you want more free channels, an API, and a simple scheduler.

Choose Zoho Social if you use Zoho CRM/Desk or need monitoring and agency plans.
